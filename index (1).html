ESTABLISHED FRAMEWORK

General Relativity

Gravity is described through the geometry of spacetime. The theory predicts gravitational waves and accurately describes many astronomical observations; its reconciliation with quantum physics remains unresolved.

ESTABLISHED FRAMEWORK

Quantum Mechanics

Quantum theory describes microscopic systems through mathematical states and probabilities. Its predictions are extraordinarily successful, while interpretations of measurement remain debated.

Stanford Encyclopedia · Quantum mechanics ↗
ACTIVE RESEARCH

Unification

The Standard Model describes three fundamental interactions; gravity is not included in that quantum framework. A complete, experimentally confirmed theory unifying gravity with quantum physics has not been established.

Emergence & Complexity
