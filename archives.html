In the fictional history of The Aetherium, a series of unusual astronomical records inspired an independent research institution. Its founders insisted that uncertainty be documented, observations reproduced, and extraordinary interpretations treated as hypotheses rather than conclusions.

The First Anomaly remains unresolved. A missing interval in the observation logs is the earliest clue that the institution's story may be stranger than its public records suggest.
