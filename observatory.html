The Observatory's First Discrepancy

Fictional canon: Station Halcyon, the observatory associated with the First Anomaly, has two archived timestamp logs that disagree by 43 seconds. Neither version has been verified as the original. The fictional survey team has marked the record unresolved and is comparing its timing against an unrelated laboratory waveform.

The investigation uses real concepts—signal calibration, timing uncertainty, reproducibility—but the station, signal, and discrepancy are fictional. This clue will become a research mission in v0.8.
