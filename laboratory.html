What are we testing?

A small body moves under Newtonian gravity from a much larger fixed central mass. Change the initial tangential speed, starting distance, and central gravitational parameter. The path responds in real time after you press Apply & reset.

Model limits: Two-body approximation with the central mass fixed; arbitrary distance and time units; no collisions, drag, relativity, or other bodies. The integration is numerical, so long simulations may accumulate error.

What are we testing?

Two ideal sine waves with the same frequency overlap. Their displacements add point by point. At zero phase difference they reinforce each other; at 180° they cancel when their amplitudes match.

Model limits: One-dimensional, coherent, equal-frequency sinusoidal signals with no damping or dispersion. This does not model a physical double-slit apparatus.

What are we testing?

A light ray meets a flat boundary between two ideal transparent media. Snell's law relates the angles through n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂. When light travels from higher to lower refractive index, total internal reflection can occur.

Model limits: Ideal geometric rays, fixed refractive indices, no absorption or wavelength-dependent dispersion. Wavelength changes the illustrative color only; it does not change the indices in this model.
